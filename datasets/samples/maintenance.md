# Synthetic maintenance handbook

This is fictional training material for software demonstrations. It is not an approved industrial procedure.

## Inspection scheduling
The fictional ALPHA-7 conveyor requires a visual inspection every 14 days. The inspection log records belt alignment, guard condition, and unusual vibration. These intervals are synthetic and must not be applied to real machinery.

## Quality documentation
In this fictional facility, quality reports record the component identifier, inspection date, defect category, inspector identifier, and image reference. The sample defect categories are scratches, pitting, and inclusions.

## Inventory planning
The sample reorder policy uses expected demand during supplier lead time plus safety stock. Safety stock depends on demand variability, lead time, and a stated service target. Simulation results are estimates under assumptions, not actual cost savings.
