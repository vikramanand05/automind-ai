# Synthetic quality investigation handbook — software demonstration only

This document describes a fictional component, batch and inspection process. It is not an approved operating procedure and must not be applied to real machinery.

## Pitting investigation

In this synthetic example, possible causes of pitting may include surface contamination or a fictional coating-process deviation. These possibilities are hypotheses, not verified root causes for any uploaded image or batch. A similar visual appearance alone does not establish cause.

Quarantine the reported rejected units in the fictional scenario. Verify the component and batch identifiers against an approved source, check inspection records, and reinspect representative units using an approved method. Record the reinspection outcome before considering release of any rejected stock.

## Production planning

Check recorded inventory and its observation date before estimating shortage. Exclude rejected units from usable stock only when the operator confirms that they are included in that inventory snapshot. A single image classification does not establish a batch reject rate.

Replacement lead time, recovered yield, demand forecasts and schedule shifts are assumptions or model estimates. Compare scenarios against the same original demand. Human approval records a decision inside AutoMind and never places an order or changes a production schedule.
